The Critical Problem

India's electrical goods distribution network faces a hidden crisis: structural fragility. 75% of towns depend on ONLY ONE distributor. When that distributor fails—due to bankruptcy, illness, relocation, or market shifts—the entire supply chain collapses overnight. This isn't theory. It's happening right now, causing massive retailer churn and lost revenue.

CRITICAL STATISTICS

75% Sole-Sourced Towns
100+ Retailers Lose Supply
2-7 Days To Recover
30-40% Revenue Loss

⚡ What Happens When A Distributor Fails

  • Day 0-1: Distributor goes dark (accident, illness, bankruptcy)
  • Day 1-2: 100+ retail shops discover zero stock
  • Day 2-3: Customers can't find products anywhere
  • Day 3-7: Retailers switch to competitor brands
  • Day 7+: Customer loyalty lost, permanent churn begins

💰 Financial Impact Per Incident

  • Lost Sales: ₹50,00,000 - ₹1,00,00,000 per town
  • Retailer Churn: 30-50% of retail base leaves
  • Recovery Time: 2-4 weeks to restore normalcy
  • Brand Damage: Customer perception hit
  • Competitor Gain: Rivals capture displaced demand

📊 The Hidden Loyalty & Revenue Problem

Beyond immediate supply loss, distributor failures create a cascade of loyalty program breakdowns:

Lost Loyalty Points: When supply stops, retailers can't earn/redeem points. 80% of loyalty participants churn.
Damaged Retailer Relationships: Retailers lose faith in the supply chain. New distributor appointment takes weeks.
Order Frequency Collapse: Shops stop placing orders when product isn't available. Habit is broken permanently.
Average Order Value Drop: When supply resumes, retailers order defensively (small quantities). Revenue per order plummets 40-50%.
Demand Signal Loss: No orders = no demand data. Next 3 months are a blind spot for forecasting.
Retailer Activation Rate Crash: % of active retailers drops from 85% to 20% in just 48 hours.

How Sanchay Works

Sanchay breaks down sales problems into 5 independent factors. Instead of "sales went down," it analyzes WHY by examining supplier count, customer demand, order frequency, pricing, and supply chain health separately. Then it tells you exactly what to fix.

Five-Factor Analysis

🏭 Suppliers: How many distributors you have

📊 Demand: How many shops want to buy

📈 Frequency: How often shops order

💰 Pricing: Average sale per shop

🔗 Pipeline: Supply vs. Demand balance
