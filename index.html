📋 Sample Data Format

Here's exactly what data you need and how to format it:

town_id division month sales distributors retailers activation frequency avg_value
T00001 AC 2024-01 150000 2 45 0.85 12 3500
T00001 AC 2024-02 145000 2 45 0.83 11 3400

Column Definitions:
  • town_id: Town identifier
  • division: Product category (AC, Lighting, Fans, etc.)
  • month: Date (YYYY-MM format)
  • sales: Total sales in rupees
  • distributors: Number of suppliers
  • retailers: Number of retail shops
  • activation: % shops that bought (0-1)
  • frequency: Orders per month per shop
  • avg_value: Average sale value per shop

📚 How Sanchay Works

Sanchay uses AI to break down sales problems into 5 independent factors. Instead of just looking at "sales went down," it analyzes WHY by examining supplier count, customer demand, order frequency, pricing, and supply chain health separately.

🔬 Five-Factor Analysis

  • Suppliers: How many distributors you have
  • Demand: How many shops want to buy
  • Frequency: How often shops order
  • Pricing: Average sale per shop
  • Pipeline: Supply vs. Demand balance
