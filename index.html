Sanchay analyzes 5 independent factors in your sales data to pinpoint exactly what caused the collapse:

  • 🏭

    Suppliers

  • 📊

    Demand Signal

    How many retailers actively want to buy

    Order Frequency

    How often retailers are placing orders
